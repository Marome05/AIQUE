The culture

Do great work.
Grow together.

01

Own the problem

Understand the problem, not just the task. Good decisions start with context.

02

Work in the open

Share unfinished thinking early. Better products come from faster feedback and shared context.

03

Respect the details

Good craft lives in the small decisions — states, handoffs, edge cases, performance, and clarity.

04

Stay practical

We care about what can ship, what can operate, and what creates value in the real world.

05

Learn across roles

Designers learn engineering constraints. Engineers understand users. Product keeps both connected.

06

Keep improving

We treat launch as a beginning: observe, learn, refine, and make the system stronger over time.
